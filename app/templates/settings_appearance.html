Appearance

Colours for everyone who signs in. The theme (Flashbang, Dark, OLED) is per browser, in the top bar.

One accent colour for the whole app: solid buttons, links, the current page in the menu, the chosen filter or tab, checkboxes and the edge of an open card. Status badges (Release, Test build, debug, errors) keep their own colours, because the colour is what they mean. The accent is adjusted automatically in each theme so text and buttons stay readable.
